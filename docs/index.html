selfdriven is a framework for developing agency. The idea is simple: a community is a function of us — so give learners real chances to care, decide and build, and learning leads somewhere that matters.

A framework for developing agency

A society is a function of us.

So we give young people genuine opportunities to authentically facilitate a community — not to simulate it, but to actually run it.

A learning community is the perfect place to practise: governance, improving the environment, the provision of food and clothing, caring for others. Real stakes. Real growth.

Self-driven

Can initiate and act on a decision to improve.

Self-aware

As it relates to self and to others.

Value the collective

The importance of community and society.
